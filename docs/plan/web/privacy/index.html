Privacy policy

Effective date: August 2026 · Applies to the Cat Metro app for Android and this website

The short version: Cat Metro has no accounts and works offline. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, or location. The app uses a small set of standard services for crash reporting, gameplay analytics, purchases, optional notifications, and optional reward ads — described in full below. We don't sell your data.

Who we are

Cat Metro is developed by [DEVELOPER LEGAL NAME], an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact for anything in this policy: hello@catmetro.com.

What the app collects, and why

Cat Metro does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The app works fully offline; the data below is collected only when your device is online.

  • Crash and performance data (Firebase Crashlytics, by Google) — device model, OS version, and crash traces, so we can find and fix bugs.
  • Gameplay analytics — pseudonymous events such as "level completed" or "daily puzzle started", tied to a random install identifier (not your identity), so we can see where levels are too hard and improve the game.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat) — when you buy something, Google processes the payment; we never see your card details. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts against a random app user ID so your purchases can be restored on reinstall.
  • Notifications (OneSignal) — only if you allow notifications: a push token and pseudonymous tags (for example, whether today's puzzle is done) so reminders you asked for can be sent. You can turn notifications off any time in system settings or in-game.
  • Reward ads (Google AdMob) — Cat Metro shows ads only when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward. There are no forced ads. When you opt to watch, Google may process your device's advertising ID to serve and measure the ad, subject to the consent choices shown in your region.

What we don't do

  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing for cross-app advertising beyond what the optional reward ads described above involve.
  • No collection of precise location, contacts, messages, or files.
  • No accounts, passwords, or profiles.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies: purchases and their restoration are processed to perform our contract with you; crash reporting and aggregate analytics rest on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it; notifications and ad-related processing rest on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Children

Cat Metro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

Crash and analytics data are retained on the providers' standard schedules and then deleted or aggregated. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to keep your purchases restorable. Since the app has no accounts, uninstalling removes all local data. To request deletion of any data associated with your device's random identifiers, email hello@catmetro.com from the device in question and we will handle it within 30 days.

Write to hello@catmetro.com; we do not discriminate for exercising these rights. California residents: we do not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.
