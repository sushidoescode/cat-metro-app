Line 1 · Executive verdict

Build the routing puzzle. Rename it. Rebalance the plan around five verified facts the prior research got wrong or missed.

The prior blueprint’s core recommendation survives audit: a one-thumb, deterministic, fixed-tick route-switching puzzle with cat commuters is the highest risk-adjusted-EV concept for a solo Unity developer in this window. The whitespace is real: no cat-themed metro or route-switching puzzle exists on Google Play (four store searches, 31 Jul). The ancestors are gone — Trainyard delisted in 2019, Mini Motorways never shipped on Android, STATIONflow and Overcrowd are PC-only. And both genre poles leave an opening: premium transit puzzles are beloved but tiny (Railbound: 211K installs at $4.99 despite an Apple Design Award), while F2P routing hits are enormous but resented (Arrows: 103.6M installs in 12 months with “ad every other level” backlash; Bus Traffic Fever: 15.4M installs, 3.72★).

Five corrections now bound the plan: ① “Loopline” is renamed Cat Metro — the old name collides on both stores and catmetro.com/.io/.app are all free. ② The Grand Prize shortlist is literally total revenue reported in RevenueCat — the catalog was repriced upward. ③ OneSignal Journeys are plan-gated (Free = 1, Growth $19/mo = 3) — the 7-journey design was unbuildable and is now a 3-journey architecture. ④ #BuildInPublic pays $30k and Samsung’s award is non-cash — priorities flipped. ⑤ Unity 6000.3.17f1+ ships AGP 9 (SDK breakage) — pin 6000.3.16f1.

Launch target: public 1.0 on Google Play, Aug 24–28. Feasible only because the 12-tester / 14-day closed test starts Aug 1–2 — the store-access clock, not the game, is the critical path.

Line 2 · The game

Cat Metro — one thumb, sixty seconds, every line moving

Portrait puzzle: tap two-state junction switches to route color-and-symbol-coded cat commuter trains to matching stations before platform queues overflow. 45–90-second levels, instant retry, cause-first failure camera. A pure-C# fixed-tick simulation (8 ticks/s, seeded, command-logged — no physics, no NavMesh) makes every level solver-validated, replayable, and AI-generatable behind deterministic quality gates.

  • Launch scope: 30 curated levels across 6 districts · 4 mechanics (switch, queue, second source, wildcard) · seeded shared Daily Line · share-card + challenge deep link · full offline play.
  • Monetization — “fair by design”: $6.99 All Access (bonus district, both premium themes, daily rewind, permanently zero non-rewarded ads) · $9.99 Supporter Pack · $2.99 themes · $1.99/$4.99 rewind packs · rewarded ads only when the player asks. No interstitials, no energy, no loot boxes, no subscription (formally rejected — no honest recurring value a solo dev can sustain in-window).
  • Stack: Unity 6000.3.16f1 · URP · IL2CPP/ARM64 · API 24→36 · purchases-unity 9.7.0 + RevenueCatUI · OneSignal 5.3.2 · Google Mobile Ads 11.3.0 · one EDM4U (1.2.188).
  • Awards: P0 — Best Game, HAMM, #BuildInPublic, OneSignal, Catvertising. P1 — Design, Grand Prize, Most Viral. P2 — Stripe Funnel (Day-35 gate), Galaxy. Skip — Replit, JetBrains, influencer categories.

Cut deliberately: starter pack, theme bundle (decoy), currency packs, streak-saver IAP, season pass, cosmetic club, any subscription. First paywall only after the Level-5 win; never after a first failure; payers fully suppressed from offers. Rewarded placements (5, all opt-in, all tracked in RC AdTracker): failure rewind, ticket doubler, daily-gift doubler, streak saver, theme rental. Zero interstitials is the Catvertising thesis — “ads only when the player asks,” measured end-to-end.

Organic base case ≈ 3k installs and ~$500–900 net revenue by Sep 30 — inside the verified range where 2025 category awards were won ($1–2k), below Grand Prize scale unless a creative breaks out. Full auditable scenarios: data/revenue_scenarios.csv.
