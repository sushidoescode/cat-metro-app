## 1. Executive Verdict

**Build the routing puzzle. Rename it. Rebalance the plan around five verified facts the prior research got wrong or missed.**

The core recommendation of the prior blueprint survives audit: a one-thumb, deterministic, fixed-tick route-switching puzzle with cat commuters is the highest risk-adjusted-EV concept available to a solo Unity developer in this window, and the market whitespace is real and verified — **no cat-themed metro/route-switching puzzle exists on Google Play** (four store searches, 31 Jul 2026), the mechanical ancestors are absent (Trainyard delisted 2019, Mini Motorways never shipped on Android, STATIONflow/Overcrowd PC-only), and the two poles of the genre both leave an opening: premium transit puzzles are beloved but tiny (Railbound: 211K installs at $4.99 despite an Apple Design Award), while F2P routing hits are enormous but hated for their monetization (Arrows – Puzzle Escape: 103.6M installs in 12 months with "ad every other level" backlash; Bus Traffic Fever: 15.4M installs, 3.72★).

But the prior plan ships with five material defects, now corrected:

1. **The name is wrong.** "Loopline" collides with an exact-match Play app, an exact-match iOS app, an actively-developed itch.io game, and a Berlin SaaS holding loopline.com — and it's a weak, crowded ASO query. **"Cat Metro"** is collision-free on every store, `catmetro.com/.io/.app` are all unregistered (registry RDAP, 31 Jul), and it is instantly descriptive.
2. **The Grand Prize is a revenue contest.** The now-published Official Rules state the shortlist is built from "the total revenue generated by eligible Projects during the Submission Period, as reported in RevenueCat." Monetization capacity matters more than the prior plan assumed; the catalog is repriced upward ($6.99 All Access + $9.99 Supporter Pack).
3. **The OneSignal plan was unbuildable.** Journeys are plan-gated: Free = 1 active journey, Growth ($19/mo) = 3. The prior 7-journey design is replaced by a 3-journey architecture (+ in-app messages + local notifications) on the Growth plan — and that constraint becomes the "resourcefulness" story the OneSignal award explicitly judges.
4. **#BuildInPublic pays $30k** — double the other categories — and Samsung's award is **non-cash** in 2026. Build-in-public becomes a P0 daily workstream; Galaxy Store drops to P2.
5. **The Unity version pin was a landmine.** Unity 6000.3.17f1+ silently switched to Gradle 9/AGP 9.0.0, which breaks unverified third-party SDKs. Pin **6000.3.16f1** (Gradle 8.13/AGP 8.10.0) until AdMob/RevenueCat/OneSignal confirm AGP9 compatibility.

Launch target: **public 1.0 on Google Play, Aug 24–28** — verified feasible only if the 12-tester/14-day closed test starts **Aug 1–2** (the closed-test clock plus production-access review plus first-release review consumes 3–5 weeks on a new personal account; this is the critical path, not the game).

## 2. Definitive Game Recommendation

**CAT METRO** (store title candidate: "Cat Metro: Train Puzzle", ≤30 chars)

Portrait, one-thumb: tap two-state junction switches to route color-and-symbol-coded cat commuter trains to matching stations before platform queues overflow. 45–90-second levels; instant retry; cause-first failure camera. Pure-C# fixed-tick simulation (8 ticks/s, seeded, command-logged) — no physics, no NavMesh — so every level is solver-validated, replayable, and AI-generatable with deterministic quality gates.

- **Launch scope:** 30 curated levels (6 districts), 4 mechanics (switch, queue capacity, second source, wildcard), seeded shared Daily Line (unlocks at L7), share-card + challenge deep link, 3 visual themes, full offline play.
- **Monetization (Model B, "fair by design"):** free game complete on its own; $6.99 All Access (bonus district + both premium themes + daily rewind + permanently zero non-rewarded ad surfaces); $9.99 Supporter Pack; $2.99 themes; $1.99/$4.99 rewind packs; rewarded ads **only when the player asks** (rewind, double tickets, streak saver, theme rental) — no interstitials, no banners, no energy, no loot boxes, no subscription. This positioning attacks the single loudest complaint in every verified competitor's reviews.
- **Retention:** Daily Line + streak badge + weekly District Cup from Week 5, powered by a 3-journey OneSignal architecture with explicit exits and in-app frequency caps.
- **Stack:** Unity 6000.3.16f1, URP, IL2CPP/ARM64, min API 24 / target API 36; purchases-unity 9.7.0 (+RevenueCatUI), OneSignal Unity 5.3.2, Google Mobile Ads Unity 11.3.0, EDM4U 1.2.188 (single copy).
- **Award targets:** P0 = Best Game, HAMM, #BuildInPublic, OneSignal, Catvertising. P1 = Design, Grand Prize, Most Viral. P2 = Stripe Funnel Vision (Day-35 gate), Galaxy (non-cash). Skip = Replit, JetBrains, influencer categories.

Full spec: `specs/product_spec.md`. Full catalog: `data/monetization_catalog.csv`.

## 6. Concept Comparison & Final Decision

Six concepts scored on the brief's 24 criteria (23 scored 1–10; #24 overall EV = the weighted total). Weights sum to 100; full per-criterion table and rationale in `_working_concept_analysis.md` (drafted before verification to avoid anchoring; scores locked after).

Weights: comprehension 5, fun-30s 5, depth 4, solo feasibility 7, Unity risk 5, content burden 5, AI pipeline 3, visual distinction 4, video 5, organic 4, ASO 4, RC fit 6, rewarded fit 4, OneSignal fit 4, live-ops 3, ethics 4, Best Game 4, Design 3, HAMM 3, Catvertising 3, Grand traction 4, launch-by-D28 7, polish-by-D56 4.

| Concept | Weighted score | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **C1 Cat Metro (deterministic route-switching)** | **8.08** | **BUILD** |
| C4 "Meowmelon" cat merge-drop (Suika-like) | 7.10 | Strong loop, fails differentiation/judge narrative (clone swarm) |
| C2 ChronoRoute: Tokyo Express | 6.70 | Dominated variant of C1 (drag input, weaker theme, interstitial/energy baggage) |
| C5 "Whisker Watch" daily deduction | 6.60 | Fine floor; traction ceiling + weak HAMM/Catvertising surface |
| C6 "Cat Snack Line" idle café | 6.19 | Best raw monetization, worst feasibility+ethics narrative |
| C3 CineCraft: Director's Cut | 5.61 | Asset volume + subjective scoring kill it; **removed even as Plan B** |

**Plan B change:** the prior blueprint's pivot target (CineCraft) is replaced by **C4 Meowmelon** — if the Day-7 fun gate fails, a merge-drop pivot reuses the entire commercial/retention/analytics stack, ships even faster, and its weaknesses (differentiation) matter less than not shipping.

**Red-team verdicts on Cat Metro** (details argued in `specs/product_spec.md`):
- *Switch intuitiveness:* one cognitive step above tap-to-release arrow games (103M installs prove the input class); mitigated by live route-highlighting from every switch and a hand-guided first tap. Risk accepted, tested at D7 gate.
- *Watchability:* the sim is juice-dependent — P0 juice list is gate-checked, not aspirational. This is the plan's biggest genuine product risk (Medium).
- *Fair failure:* cause-first failure camera + no fail-then-paywall rule.
- *Repeat sessions post-campaign:* daily seed + score chase + weekly cup is adequate for a 9-week window; year-2 depth explicitly out of scope.
- *Handcrafted vs procedural:* both — generator+solver proposes, humans curate; 30 hand-approved at launch.
- *Cat attachment:* commuters get 6 reusable reaction poses + conductor cat; attachment is a cosmetics engine, not a narrative burden.
- *Juvenile risk:* "premium tabletop diorama, not mascot clutter" art rule + 13+ targeting (also a verified Families-policy requirement).
- *Six-second ad:* "One tap saves the 8:15 to Whisker Junction" — near-overflow save is inherently 6-second legible.
- *IAP without friction:* content (district), expression (themes), convenience (rewind) — none manufacture pain.
- *AI asset achievability:* modular low-poly + one shader family verified as the standard Meshy/Tripo-viable pipeline; fallback primitives.
- *Name:* resolved — renamed Cat Metro.

**Decision: KEEP THE CONCEPT, MATERIALLY REDESIGNED** (name, pricing, retention architecture, ad stack, Unity pin, launch mechanics trimmed 4-not-6, Plan B swapped). Not unchanged; not hybridized; not replaced.
