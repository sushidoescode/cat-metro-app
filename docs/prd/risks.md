## K. Honesty section

**Attack classes checked against this PRD and found adequately handled — no register row needed:** daily-seed manipulation and clock tampering (CM-R11.5/11.6 handle rollback and jump correctly and punish display only, never the player) · pay-to-win in competitive surfaces (CM-R47.1's command-log rewind detection with cosmetic-only rewards is the right containment with no server; forged medals are cosmetic and affect no one else) · forced-ad and dark-pattern abuse of the player (CM-R19.2, CM-R25.5, CM-R26.4, CM-R32.5, CM-R36 are unusually strong and internally consistent — no exit-intent, no fake urgency, no auto-present) · PII in the share card (there is no username or account; the surface is genuinely empty) · raw price and transaction-id handling in analytics (CM-R23.5 / CM-R45.4 are correct as written) · sub-ready scaffolding and hidden recurring charges (explicitly pre-refused in §3) · classic web injection — SQL, template, SSRF, unsafe HTTP deserialization — **not applicable at 1.0** with no server and no user-supplied URL fetching.

**One conditional:** if any server component ever appears — the CUT `ops.json` kill-file, the `rc-hooks.catmetro.io` webhook host, or the Stripe web funnel (P2, Sep 4 go/no-go) — that entire class re-opens and needs a **fresh threat model before code**.

**Candidate objections examined and deliberately not raised as risks** (recorded so they are not re-litigated): Bus Jam Meow as a competitive threat (~1k installs, match-3 — dismissed); Devpost prize-table changes (re-checked, unchanged); the Unity pin (googleads-mobile-unity #4212 still open ⇒ pin holds); purchases-unity still at 9.7.0. Perishable pins were re-checked on 2026-08-02 and retired, not carried as open risk.

**No legal or regulatory blocker was found.** The venture critique states this explicitly, and the threat model concurs: the consent gap (RK-11) is a compliance *gap* with two cheap mitigations, not an illegality. Two items remain open from the audit's own unverifiable list and are unchanged: the USPTO/EUIPO trademark screens for "Cat Metro" and "Meowtro" (`AUDIT_FINDINGS.md:565-567`), and the promo-codes × purchases-unity coexistence test. Neither is closable by an agent.

**Every mitigation in this document is a proposal to be commissioned, not a control anyone has observed.** No code exists.
